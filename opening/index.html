Knowledge Representation and Learning for Smart Agriculture
PhD Open Funded

This project focuses on modeling, integrating, and applying agricultural knowledge for intelligent decision-making in digital farming systems. The research sits at the intersection of knowledge representation, machine learning, and smart agriculture — with the goal of enabling AI-driven insights for next-generation food systems.

Requirements
  • Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, or a closely related field
  • Proficiency in programming (Python, C++/C#, or MATLAB mandatory)
  • Experience with ontology engineering or knowledge graph construction
  • Background in machine learning / deep learning with structured or unstructured data
  • Ability to work with agricultural data fusion and multi-source datasets
  • Strong work ethic, communication skills, and ability to collaborate in a team environment
Research Areas
  • 3D/4D printing and additive manufacturing
  • Industry 4.0 and smart manufacturing
  • Sustainable manufacturing and circular economy
  • CAD/CAM and computational design
  • Machine learning and artificial intelligence

How to Apply

To apply for any position, please send an email to diimlab2020@gmail.com with the following information:

  1. Specify your position — Indicate the position type you are applying for (undergraduate, MASc, PhD, postdoc, or visiting) and the specific research area you are interested in.
  2. Summarize your experience — Briefly describe your prior research experience, technical skills, and any relevant projects or publications that demonstrate your qualifications.
  3. Note external funding — If you have or are applying for any external scholarships or fellowships, please mention them. This significantly strengthens your application.
  4. Attach your CV — Include your GPA, research background, technical expertise, and a list of publications (if any).

Admission Information

Prospective students are encouraged to review the official graduate admission requirements of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Guelph. The following are general guidelines:

  • Minimum GPA: 75% or above for MSc and PhD applicants
  • English proficiency: IELTS minimum 6.5 overall, or TOEFL minimum 89 iBT
  • Application deadlines: January 1, May 1, and August 1
  • Domestic tuition: Approximately $9,000 per year
  • International tuition: Approximately $21,000 per year
