Teaching

Courses offered by Dr. Sheng Yang at the University of Guelph.

Courses
ENGG 6090
Additive Manufacturing

An advanced course covering the fundamentals of additive manufacturing processes and materials. Topics include the AM workflow, process selection criteria, design for additive manufacturing, cost analysis, sustainability considerations, business models, and ethical dimensions of emerging manufacturing technologies.

ENGG 6290
Digital Manufacturing

Explores the landscape of cyber-physical manufacturing systems. Topics include augmented and virtual reality in production, artificial intelligence and big data analytics, Internet of Things, cloud computing platforms, and digital twin technologies for intelligent manufacturing environments.

ENGG 1500
Engineering Analysis

A foundational engineering mathematics course. Topics include matrix algebra, vector spaces, linear systems of equations, linear transformations, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, matrix diagonalization, complex-variable algebra, and multivariable functions relevant to engineering applications.

ENGG 3120
Computer-Aided Design & Manufacturing

Theory and practice of CAD/CAM systems. Topics include solid geometry modeling, complex shape modeling, assembly design, finite element analysis for stress and thermal problems, G-code programming, and computer-aided manufacturing simulation for production planning.
